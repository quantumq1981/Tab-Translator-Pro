TAB·DECODER workbench shell

A responsive frame for TabTranslator Pro on phone, iPad and tablet: four destinations, a four-control chart toolbar, and the chord readout in a sheet or a side panel. Layout follows the window width, so iPad Split View gets the phone layout when it is narrow. Tap a chord, open ⋯ Settings or Share, and switch sizes below. The chart is the opening of Blue Sky, the repo's validation file.

Rules the shell enforces

  • Selected = shape, not just colour: ✓ on chips, fill on tabs, aria-pressed / aria-selected.
  • Four actions in the toolbar, everything else in Settings or Share.
  • The chart scrolls with the page; only the toolbar sticks.
  • Destructive actions act at once and offer Undo (toast), never confirm().
  • Empty states say what to do next. The readout is empty until a chord is tapped.
  • Diagnostics stay behind "Details".
